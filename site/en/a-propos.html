About

One person behind VELA.

Not a big team. A project built here, in the Mauricie region of Canada — by someone who still answers his own emails.

The founder

The technology we wear should send nothing behind our backs, shut no one out, and depend on no one.

VELA is one person: me, Miguel Goufack, in Trois-Rivières. The idea started from a discomfort: the glasses we saw coming were sending everything off to a cloud. I wanted the opposite.

A camera that works when you ask for it. A light that comes on the moment it films. Images that stay on your computer. IRIS, the assistant, runs on your machine — right down to her French voice.

Thought out and written here, in French first. Whatever is still coming, I mark clearly. What I promise: when you write to VELA, a real person answers. Often, that person is me.

— Miguel Goufack, founder of VELA · Trois-Rivières, Mauricie

The discipline

The proof, not the promise.

We only state what is shipped. The rest is marked "coming". No date we're not sure we can keep.
