About

One person behind VELA.

Not a big team. A project built here, in the Mauricie region of Canada — by someone who still answers his own emails.

The founder

The technology we wear shouldn’t send anything behind our backs, shut anyone out, or depend on anyone.

VELA is one person: me, Miguel Goufack, in Trois-Rivières. The idea came from a sense of unease: the glasses we saw coming routed our data through a cloud. I wanted the opposite.

Your computer is the brain; the glasses are the interface. IRIS isn’t a chatbot: it’s a presence that lives on your computer, remembers you and grows with you — right down to its French voice, produced on the computer. And it keeps a log that you verify yourself.

A first generation, written here, French first. What isn’t ready, I mark “In testing” or “Planned”, like photos by voice, still in testing. And when you write to VELA, a real person answers. Often, it’s me.

— Miguel Goufack, founder of VELA · Trois-Rivières, Mauricie

The discipline

The proof, not the promise.

Three statuses, the same across the whole site: Available, In testing, Planned. No date we aren’t sure we can keep.

Available in IRIS 0.1.0

  • IRIS on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), included with the glasses.
  • IRIS’s French voice, produced on the computer, without Internet.
  • Everyday commands typed in French in the text chat, routines and reminders.
  • Local memory and a verifiable log.
  • Consent by type of data, private mode and local mode.

Not here yet

  • In testing“Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.
  • In testingThe glasses linked to IRIS: mic, voice in your ear, battery, photos by voice.
  • In testingIRIS Online: open-ended answers, web search, screen reading.
  • PlannedThe phone, IRIS on macOS and Linux, IRIS Online’s paid tiers, team governance.
  • PlannedThe results of the first testers, published with their consent, failures included.
