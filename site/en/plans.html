The quota

No surprise bills.

Once the online AI service opens:

  • Each request to an agent counts as one request for the month.
  • At 80% and 95% of the quota, IRIS lets you know.
  • Beyond that, she says so and stops: no overage billed.
  • The counter resets to zero each month.

One subscription, one month at a time. To change, pause or stop it, write to us. If it stops, IRIS returns to the Free plan without deleting anything.
