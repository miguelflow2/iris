IRIS, included

What doesn’t depend on any subscription.

In IRIS 0.1.0, on your computer, with no subscription:

  • AvailableIRIS’s French voice, produced on the computer, without Internet: it announces your reminders and can be tried out in the settings.
  • AvailableEveryday commands typed in French in the text chat, routines and reminders.
  • In testing“Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.
  • AvailableLocal memory, the verifiable log, consent by type of data, private mode and the 100% local mode.

The glasses rule, in IRIS 0.1.0: once your VELA glasses are paired with IRIS, IRIS only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected.

IRIS is built in French: the app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own.

IRIS Online

Nothing to set up.

No account elsewhere, no key to paste: the IRIS online service goes through a VELA relay. Each type of data sent needs your consent, and the 100% local mode blocks it.

The quota

No surprise bills.

What is written in IRIS for the IRIS Online quota:

  • Each request to IRIS Online counts as one request for the month.
  • At 80% and 95% of the quota, IRIS lets you know.
  • Beyond that, it says so and stops: no overage billed.
  • The counter resets to zero each month.

What is planned when the paid tiers open: a subscription one month at a time, which you could change or stop by writing to us. If it stops, IRIS returns to the free tier without deleting anything on your computer. The terms will be published before it opens.
