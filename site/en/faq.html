Frequently asked questions

The real questions, the real answers.

The things people ask us most, answered straight. When a service isn’t ready yet, we write « coming soon » instead of pretending. The proof, not the promise.

The proof, not the promise

Does it really work?

Does it really work, or is it just a promise?

The best proof is to try it. The IRIS app is free, and the free plan does real work: hands-free voice control of your PC, routines, reminders, local memory, offline French voice. Whatever isn’t ready yet, we label it « coming soon » instead of pretending, and the roadmap tells you where we stand.

Does IRIS work without the glasses?

Yes. The IRIS app is free and installs on a Windows PC; it works with the computer’s microphone. The glasses add a microphone and sound worn on you, plus the camera, but they aren’t required to try IRIS.

Can IRIS get things wrong?

Yes. AI answers can contain errors or omissions: check before any important decision. IRIS does not replace a professional, whether medical, legal or financial.

Local by default

Your data stays with you.

Does my data go to a cloud?

No, not by default. The core of IRIS runs on your machine: your conversations, your memory and your photos stay on the computer. The online AI service, still coming, will be optional and clearly flagged. The details are on the Privacy page.

Does it record all the time?

No. IRIS waits for the wake phrase « Dis-moi Iris »; the dialogue opens, then closes again. Speech recognition happens on the device (Vosk), and the synthetic voice too (Piper): nothing is sent anywhere else.

How does the camera work, and where do my photos go?

You take a photo or a video by voice: « Dis-moi Iris, take a photo ». An indicator light comes on the moment the camera is filming: nothing happens in secret. The images and their analysis stay on your computer — never sent to a cloud.

The computer and the systems

Where and how IRIS runs.

Does my computer have to be on?

Yes. IRIS runs on your Windows PC — it has to be on for the glasses to work. It’s a deliberate choice: the flip side of « everything stays with you ». If it’s off, IRIS doesn’t answer; it needs a computer that’s on and within range. Controlling IRIS from your phone, away from home, is Coming soon.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Honestly, not yet. Today’s installer is for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Mac and Linux are on the roadmap Coming, but they aren’t ready, and we don’t want to suggest otherwise.

What are the minimum PC requirements?

The baseline: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. For the recommended details — memory, processor — we’d rather leave them to be confirmed than throw out a number: write to us at contact@velaglass.ca and we’ll tell you honestly whether your device is a fit.

The glasses and the price

What you wear, what you pay.

Can I use my prescription lenses?

Write to us at contact@velaglass.ca: depending on your prescription, we’ll look at what’s possible together. We’d rather answer case by case than promise a single solution that wouldn’t suit everyone.

How much does it cost, and do I have to subscribe?

The glasses cost 250 CAD, a single payment, taxes extra. The IRIS app is free. The subscription that unlocks the online AI service is optional and Coming soon: never a forced subscription.

There’s also an optional « Glasses + 12 months Pro » offer at 489.88 CAD, alongside the glasses on their own at 250. The details are on the Plans page.

Try, stop, reach us

Get started and stay in control.

How do I try IRIS?

Download the free app from the Install page, on a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit). No card, no paid account: the free plan starts right away.

How do I cancel or stop IRIS?

« Stop » cuts off the speech, « mute » closes the microphone, and you can quit the app at any time. The subscription that will unlock the online AI service (coming soon) will be cancellable at any time: IRIS then returns to the free plan without deleting any of your data.

How do I reach you?

Write to contact@velaglass.ca or call 819 524-2804. A real person answers, and will tell you honestly what already works and what isn’t ready yet.
