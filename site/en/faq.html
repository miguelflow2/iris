The proof, not the promise

Does it really work?

Does it really work today?

Yes, for a specific list, and we write it down before you buy. In IRIS 0.1.0, the released version for Windows 10 and 11, the following are Available: the French voice on the computer, everyday commands typed in French in the text chat, routines and reminders, local memory, the verifiable log, consent by type of data, private mode and local mode.

In testing: “Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.

Also In testing: the glasses linked to IRIS (mic, voice in your ear, battery, photos by voice) and IRIS Online (open-ended answers, web search, screen reading). Planned: the phone, Mac and Linux.

The full table is on the What works today page, and a log produced by IRIS (scripted session) is published on the Privacy page.

What is the Founders Edition?

It is the first generation of the VELA glasses: $250 CAD, a single payment, plus taxes, IRIS included. A first generation, and we say so: the What works today page tells you what it does today and what is coming.

Payments aren’t open yet: a free waitlist is open, and no payment is taken until a delivery date is written above the payment button.

It comes with a commitment: a full refund if your glasses aren’t delivered by the delivery date written down before you paid, or if a feature marked “Available” doesn’t work as described. Only features marked “Available” are covered by this commitment; anything marked “In testing” or “Planned” is an intention, with no guaranteed date. The full text is in the terms of sale (in French).

Do I get a refund if a feature doesn’t arrive?

It depends on its status. If a feature marked “Available” doesn’t work as described, yes: a full refund, under the Founders Edition commitment.

A feature marked “In testing” or “Planned” is an intention, with no guaranteed date: it is not covered by this commitment. That is why we write it down before you buy, so you can decide with full knowledge. Your legal warranties apply in every case.

Does IRIS work without the glasses?

IRIS is made to be used with the VELA glasses. Without them, the downloaded app only offers a preview. And once your glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected. IRIS does not replace the glasses.

Can IRIS get things wrong?

Yes. AI answers can contain errors or omissions: check before any important decision. IRIS does not replace a professional, whether medical, legal or financial.

Your data

What stays with you, what is sent.

Does nothing really go out to the Internet?

Not unconditionally, and we would rather say so. In local mode, your data stays on your computer. IRIS then blocks sends to online services, and only the features handled on the computer respond: wake word, voice, everyday commands, routines, reminders, memory.

When you turn on an online feature, IRIS shows you what will be sent before it goes. In practice, in IRIS 0.1.0: each type of data (the text of your requests, images, screenshots, memory excerpts, audio) needs your consent, off by default; an attached screenshot is shown to you before it is sent; and these sends are recorded in the log. To answer freely, IRIS Online receives at least the text of your request.

What is sent without going through these consents. Outside local mode, IRIS also contacts VELA’s server at startup, with a device identifier (and your purchase email, if you entered it), to open online access, then once a day to check your plan if that email is entered; these contacts are not recorded in the log. The 100% local mode blocks them.

The details, mechanism by mechanism, are in the privacy policy (in French).

Does my data go to a cloud?

Memory, history and the log are stored on your computer, and consents to send are off by default. IRIS Online is optional and In testing: your requests, attached images, screenshots and memories aren’t sent without your consent, type by type, and these sends are recorded in the log.

Outside local mode, IRIS also contacts VELA’s server at startup, with a device identifier (and your purchase email, if you entered it), to open online access; that contact is not recorded in the log. The 100% local mode blocks it. See the Privacy page.

Does it record all the time?

No. IRIS listens to spot “Dis-moi Iris” (with the glasses, In testing); that detection happens on the computer, with a French model installed with the app, and that sound is not sent online. The dialogue opens, then closes. IRIS’s voice is also produced on the computer.

One exception, which you choose: if you turn on online recognition yourself (off by default), the audio of your requests is sent, and these sends are recorded in the log.

How does the camera work, and where do my photos go?

The camera is part of the glasses. Photos by voice with IRIS are In testing: IRIS 0.1.0 still refuses to trigger the camera until the command is confirmed on the real hardware. What is written for that day: the image is brought back to your computer, and the capture is recorded in the log. Video by voice is Planned. The frame has an indicator light next to the camera.

The computer and systems

Where and how IRIS runs.

What if I leave without my computer?

Without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today. It is the computer that listens, answers and acts: your computer is the brain, VELA is your interface. Driving IRIS from your phone, away from the computer, is Planned: an iPhone app is in preparation, with no date.

Why only Windows?

Because it is the only released and verified version: IRIS 0.1.0, for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, Available. Controlling the computer and the link with the glasses rely first on Windows. For Mac and Linux, IRIS still has to be built and tested on each system: they are Planned, with no date, and we don’t want to suggest otherwise.

Does IRIS work in English?

IRIS is built in French. The app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands, the ones handled on the computer, are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own. The statuses on this site describe IRIS in French.

What are the minimum PC requirements?

The basics: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Memory and processor guidelines are on the Install page, marked “to be confirmed”: they haven’t been measured on a test bench yet. If in doubt, write to us at contact@velaglass.ca: we will tell you frankly whether your device is suitable.

Try, stop, reach us

Getting started, and staying in control.

How do I try IRIS?

Download IRIS from the Install page, on a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit). No card required. Without VELA glasses, it is a preview: IRIS is designed and sold to be used with them.

How do I mute or stop IRIS?

“Mute” cuts the mic, so does private mode, and you can close the app whenever you like. The 100% local mode blocks sends to online services. No subscription is sold today: there is nothing to cancel.
