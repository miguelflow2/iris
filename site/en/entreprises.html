Straight talk

What works, what’s being built.

  • Available on each Windows 10 or 11 workstation, in IRIS 0.1.0: the French voice on the computer, everyday commands typed in French in the text chat, routines, reminders, local memory, verifiable log, consents, private mode and local mode.
  • In testing “Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.
  • In testing the glasses linked to IRIS, and IRIS Online (open-ended answers, dictation, search, screen reading).
  • The rule in IRIS 0.1.0: once VELA glasses are paired with a workstation, IRIS there only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected.
  • Planned team governance (workstation management, grouped billing, deployment), the paid tiers including the Enterprise plan, the phone, Mac and Linux. The details are on the What works today page.

IRIS is built in French: the app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own.

A custom quote? The price of $99.99 per month is under consideration for the Enterprise plan, which isn’t open. For a fleet of several dozen workstations, write to us: we agree together on what is fair, without inflating the quote.
