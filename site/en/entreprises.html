Where it helps

Uses that add up as the team grows.

IRIS doesn't replace anyone. It removes friction — hands busy, fatigue, the barrier of the screen — wherever voice saves time.

Home and community care

Dictate a note, open a file, set a reminder — by voice, hands free during care. The camera documents an action on command; the image stays on the workstation, and an indicator lights up as soon as it's recording.

Rehabilitation centres

For people with reduced mobility, everything is done by voice: writing, searching, navigating, setting reminders. IRIS adapts to each person's pace and repeats without losing patience.

Employers and workplaces

A shared tool across several workstations, with clear billing. Useful when hands are full — workshop, warehouse, lab, service floor — or for anyone who would rather dictate than type.

Workplace accessibility

A way into the workstation for someone with low vision, who struggles to write or tires quickly. The French voice runs locally: nothing forces you to hand a diagnosis to a third-party service.
