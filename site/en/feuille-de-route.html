What works today

What works today, and what’s coming.

The question we get most often: “does it really work, or is it a vision?”. Here is the answer, feature by feature, for the released version of IRIS 0.1.0 and the VELA glasses of the Founders Edition. No date we aren’t sure we can keep.

How to read it

Three statuses, the same across the whole site, and nothing else:

  • Available in the released IRIS 0.1.0 installer, and verifiable
  • In testing written, but not yet proven on the real glasses, or not yet released
  • Planned an intention, with no guaranteed date

When in doubt, we write “In testing” or “Planned”, never “Available”. A feature that fails our trials, or those of our first testers, drops down a status, and this table is corrected. Every correction is dated in the table history, at the bottom of the page.

Without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today. IRIS runs on your Windows computer: that is what listens, answers and acts. The glasses are the interface. The phone app is Planned.

The glasses rule, in IRIS 0.1.0. Once your VELA glasses are paired with IRIS, IRIS only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected. And the glasses’ link with IRIS is In testing.

What the Founders Edition commitment covers: only the “Available” rows

A full refund if your glasses aren’t delivered by the delivery date written down before you paid, or if a feature marked “Available” doesn’t work as described. Only features marked “Available” are covered by this commitment; anything marked “In testing” or “Planned” is an intention, with no guaranteed date.

No payment is taken today: payments will only open with a “Delivery no later than…” date written right above the payment button. This commitment comes on top of your legal warranties; its full text (refund, timelines, return): terms of sale · warranty and returns (in French).

Without glasses, the IRIS app is only a preview: it is designed and sold to be used with the VELA glasses. Version 0.2.0 is in development and has not been released: nothing it contains is counted as “Available” here.

About English. IRIS is built in French: the app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own. The “Available” rows above describe IRIS in French.
