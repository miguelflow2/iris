Roadmap

Where we really stand.

IRIS is at version 0.1.0. Here’s what already works, what we’re building right now, and what’s planned next. Our rule is simple : no date we aren’t sure we can keep. Wherever the timeline isn’t set, it says “to be confirmed”.

Current version IRIS 0.1.0 Windows 10 and 11 (64 bit) · Free plan, on your computer
How to read this page

Three states, and nothing else :

  • Done — what runs today
  • In progress — what we’re working on
  • Planned — what comes next

For everything marked “Planned”, the target quarter is to be confirmed : we won’t announce it until we’re sure we can keep it.

Done Available

What runs today.

IRIS version 0.1.0, on your computer, with the Free plan. None of this is a promise : it’s what the app already does.

  • Controlling your computer by voice : open, search, type, launch, remind.
  • The wake word “Dis-moi Iris” and follow-up dialogue, without repeating it.
  • Routines and reminders.
  • Memory kept on the device : your preferences and your decisions.
  • The local French voice (Piper), which works offline.
  • Local speech recognition (Vosk), on your computer.
  • Privacy : conversations, memory and logs stay with you, with consent per data type and a capture indicator.

In progress At work

What we’re building right now.

The Windows installer, with the voice built in

Packaging the app so it installs in a single click, local French voice included, with no technical steps at all. In progress

The online AI service

The service that sets the paid tiers apart (Pro, Premium, Business) and widens the monthly quota. It’s what rolls out behind the “Coming soon” note on the plans. In progress

The first batch of glasses

Preparing the very first units and their delivery, arranged by email with each person. In progress

Planned Next

What’s coming.

None of these dates are locked in. The target quarter is marked “to be confirmed” until we’re certain : we’d rather say so plainly than announce a date we couldn’t keep.

Opening the paid tiers to everyone

Pro, Premium and Business available to all, once the online service is proven. Planned — target quarter to be confirmed

The fully local, self-reliant mode

Answering and searching without depending on an online service : everything on your machine, even cut off from the Internet. Planned — target quarter to be confirmed

Team governance (Enterprise plan)

Managing several workstations, grouped billing and deployment for organizations. Planned — target quarter to be confirmed

macOS and Linux

Bringing IRIS beyond Windows. A good part of the code is already suited to it ; what remains is the packaging and testing on each system. Planned — target quarter to be confirmed
