What IRIS does

Control your computer by voice.

IRIS acts on your computer, by voice, only when you ask. Here is what it can do — and, further down, what does not exist yet.

The rule, first

On command. Never on its own.

IRIS only acts once you ask. Every action is written to the local log, which you open with one click — and can export.

How verification works

How it works

Everything happens on your PC.

IRIS runs on your Windows PC — it has to be on for the glasses to work. The glasses talk to the computer over Bluetooth ; nothing goes out to the internet.

Day to day

Honesty

What does not exist yet.

We’d rather say so. None of this is counted in what you pay today.

  • Open-ended answers from the online AI service — and web browsing — are rolling out. The rest, which runs on your machine, already works.
  • Commanding IRIS from your phone, away from home. For now, a computer that is switched on has to be within range. That’s the next milestone.
  • Mac and Linux. Today’s installer is the one for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. macOS and Linux are on the roadmap.
  • Enterprise governance. Planned for the Enterprise plan : coming.
