What IRIS does

Control your computer by voice.

IRIS acts on your Windows computer when you ask it to: in the text chat today, by voice with the glasses, in testing. Each capability below carries its status: Available, In testing or Planned — the same as on the What works today page.

What works today How you can check

The rule, first

When you ask.

IRIS acts on the computer when you ask it to. The routines and reminders you have created are triggered by their phrase or at their time. The log, on your computer, records in particular consent and mode changes, purges, and sends to IRIS Online.

How it works

Your computer is the brain. VELA is your interface.

Without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today. Three levels, each in its place; the third one is optional.

1 · The glasses

VELA, your interface

Mic, open-ear speaker, camera and buttons. Connected to the computer over Bluetooth.

2 · The intelligence

IRIS, on your computer

The French voice and everyday commands work on the computer, without Internet; so does the wake word, in testing with the glasses. Memory and the log are stored there.

3 · Optional

IRIS Online

For open-ended answers and multi-step requests. Each type of data sent needs your consent; the 100% local mode blocks it.

In testing

In local mode, your data stays on your computer. When you turn on an online feature, IRIS shows you what will be sent before it goes. In practice, in IRIS 0.1.0: one consent per type of data (the text of your requests, images, screenshots, memory excerpts, audio), off by default; a preview before a screenshot is attached; and these sends recorded in the log. Outside local mode, IRIS also contacts VELA’s server at startup, with a device identifier (and your purchase email, if you entered it), to open online access; that contact is not recorded in the log, and the 100% local mode blocks it.

Day to day

You speak. It acts.

What is handled on the computer is Available. What goes through IRIS Online or through the glasses, voice included, is still In testing. The rule in IRIS 0.1.0: once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected.

“Dis-moi Iris” and dialogue

The wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing. After an answer, you carry on without repeating it. Audio is only sent online if you turn on online recognition, which is off by default.

In testing

IRIS’s French voice

Produced on the computer, without Internet: it announces your reminders and can be tried out in the settings. Answers spoken aloud after “Dis-moi Iris” are in testing, with the glasses.

Everyday commands, in the text chat

Open an installed app or a common website, play a video, ask for the time or the date: typed in French, handled on the computer. By voice: in testing, like “Dis-moi Iris”.

Routines and reminders

Created in the app: a phrase replays a routine, the reminder is spoken at the set time. Creating them from a free-form sentence goes through IRIS Online, in testing.

A local memory

Memories encrypted on your computer, which you can view, add and delete. Using them in online answers, with your consent, is in testing.

Web search

Looking up current information and summarizing it, through IRIS Online.

Photos by voice

“Prends une photo” (take a photo) with the glasses’ camera. IRIS 0.1.0 still refuses to trigger it until the command is confirmed on the real hardware.

In testing

Privacy is not a paid tier. Local memory, consent by type of data, capture indicator, private mode, 100% local mode and the verifiable log are in IRIS 0.1.0, with no subscription. IRIS Online’s paid tiers are Planned: see the plans.

IRIS is built in French: the app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own.
