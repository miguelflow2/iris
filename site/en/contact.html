What people ask us most.

Does IRIS work without the glasses ?

Yes. It is a full Windows application, used through your computer's microphone — the glasses only add the microphone and the sound within earshot.

No. The Free plan opens the moment you install. Answers from online models arrive with the service now rolling out Coming soon.
