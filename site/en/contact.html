In a nutshell

What people often ask us.

Does IRIS work without the glasses?

IRIS is made to be used with the VELA glasses: without them, the app is only a preview. Once your glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected. And without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today. What works today.

Do I have to pay to try it?

No. IRIS is a free download. IRIS Online, the optional service for open-ended answers, is In testing. IRIS is built in French.

Where does my data go?

In local mode, your data stays on your computer. When you turn on an online feature, IRIS shows you what will be sent before it goes. In practice, in IRIS 0.1.0: one consent per type of data, off by default, a preview before a screenshot is attached, and the send recorded in the log. What is sent, in detail.

How do I buy the glasses?

Founders Edition: $250 CAD, a single payment. Payments aren’t open yet: join the waitlist, free and with no commitment. The delivery date will be written down before any payment. See the offer and the commitment.
