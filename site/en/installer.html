Install IRIS.

Before you download

The setup you need.

IRIS is a Windows application that runs continuously on your computer. Here is what we recommend — to be confirmed : these are not yet numbers measured on a test bench, but honest reference points, given before you install.

It works

The recommended minimum

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • A recent 4-core processor.
  • 8 GB of memory (RAM).
  • ~1 GB of free disk space.
  • A microphone : the PC's own, the VELA glasses, or a headset.

It's comfortable

Recommended

  • Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • A recent 6-core processor or more.
  • 16 GB of memory (RAM).
  • An SSD with about 2 GB free.
  • A good-quality microphone, for more reliable listening.

Recommended, not yet "tested". These reference points will be replaced by verified numbers as soon as we have measured IRIS on several machines. If in doubt, write to us at contact@velaglass.ca : we'll tell you honestly whether your computer is a good fit.

The steps to follow

Four steps, in order.

  1. Download the file

    It's called IRIS-Setup.exe and lands in your Downloads folder.

    Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · 246 MB · version 0.1.0

  2. Start the installation

    Double-click the file. Keep the suggested folder if you're unsure. IRIS launches on its own at the end.

    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This message comes from the fact that the installer isn't signed yet ; it says nothing about the content.

  3. Answer the assistant

    Five questions, all editable later : your first name ; the email of your purchase and a password that stays on the computer ; your consents ; the microphone ; the activation word. No AI key to paste : access will come from the VELA service, currently being rolled out.

  4. Say your first sentence

    Out loud : "Dis-moi Iris, remind me in ten minutes." IRIS confirms and schedules the reminder. Requests that go through the online models will arrive as soon as the service is deployed.

Good to know

It works even without glasses.

IRIS runs on its own on a Windows PC. The computer's microphone is enough — it's even clearer than the one on a Bluetooth headset.

  • The activation word, routines, reminders and memory work from the moment you install it, on your device.
  • The local French voice (Piper) speaks offline, without a key.
  • Responses from the online AI models arrive when the service is deployed Coming soon.
