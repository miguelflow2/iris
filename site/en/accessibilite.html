Where things honestly stand. The basic commands — open, write, search, dictate, remind — work today, with local speech recognition (French is built in; the English model downloads once, about 40 MB) and a local voice (French built in; in English, your computer’s own voice). Screen reading and mouse control open up with the Premium plan; they work on common apps, not yet on all of them. The most advanced capabilities of the online AI service are Coming soon.

Why local helps

Your data stays under your control.

An accessibility tool sees a lot of your life. At VELA, it stays with you.

  • Conversations, memory, activity log — everything stays on your computer.
  • The English voice and the basic speech recognition run offline.
  • You check for yourself, with one click, what was captured and sent.
  • The trade-off we own — IRIS runs on your Windows PC, which must be on for the glasses to work.
