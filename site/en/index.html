How it works

IRIS runs on your PC. That's a choice.

IRIS runs on your Windows PC — it has to be on for the glasses to work. That's the trade-off of “ everything stays with you ” : nothing leaves for a cloud, because everything happens on your own computer.

The camera

Take the photo without reaching for your phone.

A camera in the frame. “ Dis-moi Iris, take a photo ” : an indicator light comes on the moment it records — nothing happens in secret — then the image is filed on your own computer.
