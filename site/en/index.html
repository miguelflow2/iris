3 · Optional

IRIS Online

For open-ended answers and web search. Each type of data sent needs your consent; the 100% local mode blocks these sends. It goes through a VELA server.

In testing

IRIS isn’t a chatbot: it’s a presence that lives on your computer, remembers you and grows with you.

Many smart glasses connect you to the cloud. VELA connects AI to you.

In local mode, your data stays on your computer. When you turn on an online feature, IRIS shows you what will be sent before it goes. In practice, in IRIS 0.1.0: one consent per type of data, off by default, a preview before a screenshot is attached, and the send recorded in the log. Outside local mode, IRIS also contacts VELA’s server at startup, with a device identifier; that contact is not recorded in the log (the details).

And there is a trade-off, stated plainly: without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today.

The proof, not the promise

A log produced by IRIS, verifiable in your browser.

IRIS records consent and mode changes, purges, and sends to the online service in a log chained by SHA-256 hashes. A line changed after the fact breaks the chain, unless every hash that follows is recalculated: that’s why, alongside a log produced by IRIS during a scripted test session, we publish its final hash. Check it yourself.

What works today

Before you buy, here’s where things stand.

The same labels everywhere on the site, and one rule: when in doubt, we write “In testing” or “Planned”, never “Available”.

  • Available in the released IRIS 0.1.0 installer, and verifiable
  • In testing written, but not yet proven on the real glasses, or not yet released
  • Planned an intention, with no guaranteed date
  • AvailableIRIS on Windows 10 and 11, and IRIS’s French voice, produced on the computer, without Internet: it announces your reminders and can be tried out in the settings.
  • AvailableIn the text chat, everyday commands typed in French, handled on the computer (open an app or a website, the time, the date); routines and reminders.
  • AvailableMemory and the verifiable log on your computer; consent by type of data, private mode and local mode.
  • In testing“Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.
  • In testingThe glasses linked to IRIS: their mic, IRIS’s voice in your ear, the battery, photos by voice.
  • In testingIRIS Online: open-ended answers, web search, screen reading.
  • PlannedThe phone app (iPhone in preparation), Mac and Linux, the IRIS Online subscription.

IRIS is built in French: the app’s interface is in French, and its everyday commands are understood in French. Its settings offer English (US): the English speech-recognition model then has to be downloaded once (about 40 MB). IRIS has no built-in English voice: its offline voice is French, and in the settings you can switch to the Windows voice, your computer’s own.

Without a computer that is switched on and within range, the glasses don’t drive IRIS today. And without the glasses, the IRIS app is only a preview: it is made to be used with them.

The glasses rule, in IRIS 0.1.0: once your VELA glasses are paired with IRIS, IRIS only responds, by voice or in writing, while they are connected.
