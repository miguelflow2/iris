In photos

The frame, from every angle.

No 3D render : these are photos of the real unit.

IRIS runs on your Windows PC — it has to be on for the glasses to work. That is the trade-off for « everything stays with you » : the glasses carry the mic, the sound and the camera, but it is your computer that listens, answers and stores your images.

Two versions

One frame, two lenses.

Clear for everyday, tinted for outdoors. Same mic, same sound, same camera. You choose by email after payment.

The camera

A photo, without pulling out your phone.

« Dis-moi Iris, take a photo » : the camera captures what you see, hands free. An indicator lights up the moment it records, then the image is stored on your computer.

An indicator when it records

Nothing behind your back : the people in front of you see the indicator too.

The images stay with you

On your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you ask.

The sound

A Bluetooth headset, and much more.

The mic and speakers are in the frame. Windows sees the glasses as a Bluetooth headset : reconnection is automatic.

The mic that listens for IRIS

It is how IRIS hears « Dis-moi Iris » and your request.

The sound in your ear

The IRIS voice, music, any sound from the PC. And it works as a Bluetooth headset with any app.

In the box

Three items, and a free app.

  • 1 The frame Clear or tinted lenses : you choose by email after payment.
  • 2 The USB-C charging case The brown shell : the glasses rest in it and recharge. Cable included.
  • 3 The hard case The black shell, to carry them without damage.
  • 4 The IRIS app Not in the box : a free download.

IRIS also works perfectly well without the glasses, using the PC's mic and speakers. The glasses add the voice in your ear and the camera.
