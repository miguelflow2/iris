With IRIS, today

What the glasses do with IRIS, status by status.

The same labels everywhere on the site. When in doubt, we write “In testing” or “Planned”, never “Available”.

  • Available in the released IRIS 0.1.0 installer, and verifiable
  • In testing written, but not yet proven on the real glasses, or not yet released
  • Planned an intention, with no guaranteed date
  • AvailableIRIS on your Windows 10 or 11 computer: IRIS’s French voice, everyday commands typed in French in the text chat, routines, reminders, memory and the log.
  • In testing“Dis-moi Iris”: the wake word is recognized on the computer, without Internet, but once your VELA glasses are paired, IRIS 0.1.0 only listens to you while they are connected, and that link is in testing.
  • In testingThe Bluetooth connection to the computer, and reconnection by IRIS, on pairs from the first batch.
  • In testingThe glasses’ mic for “Dis-moi Iris”, IRIS’s voice in your ear, the computer’s sound in the glasses.
  • In testingThe battery level read by IRIS. Battery life: not yet measured by VELA.
  • In testingPhotos by voice: IRIS 0.1.0 still refuses to trigger the camera.
  • PlannedVideo by voice, and driving IRIS from your phone, away from the computer.
